## Generation Settings (for all images)

**Recommended for Midjourney**:
```
--ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6
```

**Recommended for DALL-E 3**:
- Aspect ratio: Landscape (1792×1024)
- Style: Vivid
- Copy prompt exactly as written

**Recommended for Stable Diffusion XL**:
- Resolution: 1024×576 (then upscale to 1920×1080)
- Steps: 30-40
- CFG Scale: 7-8
- Sampler: DPM++ 2M Karras
- Negative prompt: "blurry, low quality, text artifacts, watermark, signature, photorealistic, 3d render"

**Color Codes Reference**:
- Teal: #5AB3B3
- Dark Gray: #1A1A1A
- Green (success): #28A745
- Yellow (warning): #FFC107
- Red (error): #DC3545

**MENTOR-9 (Teacher Robot)**:
- Larger, more angular design
- Battle-worn, experienced look
- Multiple sensor arrays
- Calm, patient demeanor

- Teal emergency lighting throughout
- Visible damage: exposed wiring, cracked panels
